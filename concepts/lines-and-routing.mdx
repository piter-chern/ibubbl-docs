A **line** is a phone number iBubbl runs for you on a dedicated Mac. Your account can have one line or several. This page explains which line a message goes out on, and whether it goes as an iMessage or a text. To see your lines, whether each is online, and how much of its [limits](/concepts/limits) it has used today, call [`GET /v1/lines`](/api-reference/lines/list).

## Each contact sticks to one line

The first time your account messages a contact, iBubbl assigns the contact to one of your lines. After that, every message to that contact goes out on the same line, so they always see the same number. The assignment never expires.

It changes in only two cases:

- **The line can no longer be used** (paused, retired or moved to another account). The next message to the contact moves them to another of your lines. See [When a conversation's line is no longer yours](#when-a-conversations-line-is-no-longer-yours).
- **The contact texts one of your other lines.** Their next message from you comes from the line they texted. Messages in a group don't move anyone.

A line whose Mac is temporarily offline keeps its contacts. Their messages wait as `queued` until it is back. See [Limits and queues](/concepts/limits#offline-lines).

### How a new contact gets a line

For a contact with no line yet, iBubbl picks among your lines that are active and whose Mac is online. It prefers a line that still has room for new contacts today, then the one that has messaged the fewest new contacts this hour, then today. If none of your lines' Macs is online, the send gets `422 no_eligible_line`.

### You can't choose the line

`POST /v1/messages` has no field for the sending line or number. Fields it doesn't know, such as `from` or `line_id`, are **ignored without an error**: the request succeeds and the line is chosen as above.

To check that a line has room before you start a batch of first messages, read its counters with [List lines](/api-reference/lines/list) or [Retrieve a line](/api-reference/lines/retrieve).

### Which line a message was on

Every message names its line, in both directions, in the [send](/api-reference/messages/send) response, [Retrieve a message](/api-reference/messages/retrieve) and [List messages](/api-reference/messages/list):

| Field | Meaning |
| ----- | ------- |
| `line_id` | The ID of the line that sent the message, or received it. The same `id` as in [`GET /v1/lines`](/api-reference/lines/list). Always set, group messages included. |
| `line_number` | That line's phone number (E.164), the line's `phone_number`. |

`to` is still the contact's number: the destination of an outbound message and the sender of an inbound one. `line_number` is your side of the conversation.

Every `message.*` [webhook](/webhooks/events#the-line_id-field) carries `line_id` too. The line's number is `from_number` on the outbound events (`message.sent`, `message.delivered`, `message.read`, `message.failed`, `message.cancelled`) and `to_number` on `message.inbound`. Tapback events (`message.reaction.*`) don't carry `line_id`: read it from the message they point to.

A [retired](/api-reference/lines/list#responses) line stays in the list, so the `line_id` on an old message always resolves.

### When a conversation's line is no longer yours

A line keeps its ID when it's paused, retired or moved to another account, so a conversation can still name a line you can no longer use. The conversation and its history stay.

- **One-to-one.** The next message to the contact goes out on another of your lines, picked as for a [new contact](#how-a-new-contact-gets-a-line). They see it as a new conversation from that number. [`conversation.line_changed`](/webhooks/events#conversation-line-changed) fires with `cause: "send"`.
- **Groups.** A group lives on its line and can't move. A send with its `chat_guid` gets `422 no_eligible_line` until someone presses **Continue** on it in the iBubbl dashboard. Continue starts the group again from another of your lines, with the same people, who see it as a new group from a different number. The conversation keeps its `conversation_id`; its `chat_guid` is `null` until the next message, sent from the dashboard, creates the new group, and the old `chat_guid` then returns `404 group_not_found`.
- **Messages sent from the old line** can't be [edited](/api-reference/messages/edit), [unsent](/api-reference/messages/unsend) or [reacted to](/api-reference/messages/react) any more: those requests get `422 no_eligible_line`.

In the dashboard, such a conversation shows a notice in place of the reply box, naming the line it will continue on.

## iMessage or SMS

For each one-to-one message to a US or Canadian number (`+1`), with or without files, iBubbl decides whether to send it as an iMessage or as a text message (SMS). It goes down this list and stops at the first step that gives an answer:

1. **The number recently failed on iMessage.** An earlier message to this number failed with iMessage error 22 (not reachable on iMessage) in the last 30 days, and no iMessage to the number has been delivered since. **→ SMS.**
2. **An iMessage reached the number recently.** An iMessage from your account to this contact was delivered in the last 30 days. **→ iMessage.**
3. **Apple answered recently.** iBubbl asked Apple whether the number uses iMessage less than 30 days ago. **→ That answer.**
4. **Ask Apple now.** Your line's Mac asks Apple whether the number uses iMessage. Yes **→ iMessage.** No, or no answer within 5 seconds **→ SMS.**

The message that failed with error 22 is not resent automatically: it stays `failed` with `failed_reason: "imessage_undeliverable"`. Step 1 makes sure your *next* message to that number goes as SMS. Send it again yourself if it still matters.

A message with [files](/guides/attachments) gets the same answer as a text. Over SMS, its files go as one MMS or as separate messages, and an Android phone with RCS gets one file per message. A PDF can't go over plain SMS, so it fails there. [Send attachments](/guides/attachments#what-your-contact-sees) shows how each kind arrives.

### What isn't routed

- **Numbers outside `+1`** always go as iMessage. If the recipient isn't on iMessage, the message fails with `imessage_undeliverable`; iBubbl never falls back to SMS for them.
- **Group messages** always go as iMessage.

Routing is on for every line, for one-to-one messages to `+1` numbers with or without files, as of 2026-11-11.

## Which channel a message used

[`GET /v1/messages/{id}`](/api-reference/messages/retrieve) returns `channel`, and so do the webhooks from [`message.sent`](/webhooks/events#the-channel-field) onwards (`message.delivered`, `message.read`, `message.failed`, `message.cancelled`), plus `message.inbound` for messages you receive:

| Value | Meaning |
| ----- | ------- |
| `imessage` | Sent (or received) as an iMessage. |
| `sms` | Sent (or received) as a text message. **RCS counts as `sms` too**: iBubbl doesn't tell SMS and RCS apart in the API. |
| `null` | Not sent yet, or, rarely, iBubbl couldn't tell how it went out. For an inbound message: received before iBubbl started recording the channel. |

Some behaviour depends on the channel. For example, a plain SMS never reports `read`. Check `channel` before assuming iMessage-only behaviour.

<Note>
Each webhook carries `channel` as it was when the event fired. `message.queued` doesn't carry it, since nothing has been sent yet. `GET /v1/messages/{id}` always returns the latest value.
</Note>
